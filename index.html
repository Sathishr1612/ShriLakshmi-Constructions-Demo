About us

Built on integrity, driven by excellence

Based in Banashankari, Bengaluru, Shri Lakshmi Constructions & Engineers designs and builds homes and commercial spaces that families and businesses can rely on for generations.

Every project combines sound engineering, quality materials and transparent dealings, from the first drawing to the day we hand over the keys. Our name reflects the prosperity and well-being we wish to bring to every home we build. Our engineers stay involved at every stage, so quality is checked on site, not just on paper.

To be Bengaluru’s most trusted name in construction, known for quality, transparency and timely delivery.

To build safe, durable and thoughtfully designed spaces using sound engineering, quality materials and honest communication with every client.
